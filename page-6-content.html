Creating Your Profile

The best LinkedIn profiles have three things:

  • A professional profile picture (profiles with photos receive 21x more profile views and 36x more messages — use a proper headshot, not a casual photo)
  • An engaging headline — this is more than your job title, and it's the first thing recruiters see
  • A compelling "About" section that tells your professional story

Headline Formula

[Your Role] + [Unique Value] + [Outcome You Deliver]

Examples:

  • "Project Manager in Healthcare Tech | Implementing Scalable Solutions | Enhancing Care Through Innovation"
  • "Project Management Student | Experienced in Construction & Infrastructure | Building Skills in Scalable Solutions for Impact"

Your headline is your elevator pitch — make it count! Use action words and focus on the value you bring, not just your title.
