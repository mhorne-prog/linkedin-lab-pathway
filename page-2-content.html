Quick Poll: Is LinkedIn for Me?

Reflect on Your LinkedIn Knowledge & Explore the Possibilities

Before we dive deeper into building your LinkedIn profile, let's take a moment to understand where you're starting from and what you already know about professional networking.

1 Do you already have a LinkedIn account?

Think about whether you've created an account before, and if so, how active you've been on the platform.

2 What do you think LinkedIn is useful for?

Consider both what you know about LinkedIn from personal experience or others, and how you think it might benefit your career in Project Management.

3 What questions do you have about LinkedIn?

Don't hold back! Whether it's about safety, privacy, how to use features, or career strategy — write down what you want to know.

💡 Why This Matters

Networking and visibility in Project Management careers is highly important. This Pathway will show you how to use LinkedIn effectively to showcase your skills and experience throughout your degree — and build genuine professional connections that matter for your career.

Your Reflection Activity 10 minutes

In this activity, you'll respond to the three poll questions above. We're not looking for perfect answers — we want to hear your honest thoughts and questions.

How to complete this activity:

  1. Question 1: Briefly explain your current LinkedIn situation (e.g., "Yes, I have an account but haven't used it much" or "No, I haven't created one yet")
  2. Question 2: List 2-3 things you think LinkedIn could be useful for, based on what you know or have heard
  3. Question 3: Write 1-2 genuine questions you have about LinkedIn (e.g., "Is LinkedIn safe for privacy?" or "How do I approach someone for a connection?")
💬 Pro Tip: This is a low-stakes reflection. Your honest answers help us understand your starting point and tailor the rest of the Pathway to your needs. There are no "wrong" answers here!
