# Page 3: Digital Presence — Activity

**Task Type:** Mark as complete

**Duration:** Allow ~7 minutes

## Task

Search yourself:
1. Google your name and review the results.
2. Check your LinkedIn profile (if you have one).
3. Ask yourself:
   - Is this professional?
   - Does it reflect your career goals?

Once you've completed the self-audit, mark this task as complete.

---

## Notes for Activity Setup

- This is a hands-on self-reflection task.
- Use a **Mark as complete** task type (students don't need to submit anything — just complete the audit themselves).
- Suggested time: ~7 minutes for a quick search and reflection.
- This primes students to think critically about their online presence before moving to the practical steps.
