# Page 6: Creating Your Profile — Activity

**Task Type:** Text response

## Task

Write your own headline using the formula provided:

**[Your Role] + [Unique Value] + [Outcome You Deliver]**

Then add it to your LinkedIn profile.

**In your response, share:**
1. Your headline (as you've written it)
2. Brief explanation of how it reflects your career goals

---

## Notes for Activity Setup

- Use a **Text response** task type.
- Students should submit their headline as text.
- Estimated time: 10–15 minutes (thinking through the formula and drafting).
- You may want to review a few headlines in feedback to give examples of strong vs. weak formulations.
- Remind students to also upload a professional headshot to their LinkedIn profile as part of this step.
