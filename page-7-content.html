Your About Section

Expand on your headline and tell your professional story. Think about your skills, experiences, and career goals. Structure it around:

  • Who you are — your background and current role
  • What you do best — your key strengths and skills
  • Key achievements — quantified results or notable projects
  • What opportunities you're looking for — your career direction and ideal next step

Remember: Your About section is read by recruiters, peers, and potential connections. Make it authentic, specific, and forward-looking — not a generic summary.
