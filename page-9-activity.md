# Page 9: Connections & Next Steps — Activity

**Task Type:** Mark as complete

## Task

Send a connection request (with a personal note) to at least one classmate, colleague, lecturer, or industry professional.

**How to:**
1. Click the **"Connect"** button on someone's LinkedIn profile
2. Click **"Add a note"** to personalize your request
3. Keep your note brief (2–3 sentences) and authentic — mention something you have in common or why you'd like to connect
4. Send it

**Example note:**
> "Hi [Name], I've been impressed by your work in [area] and would love to connect and learn more about your experience in Project Management. Looking forward to staying in touch!"

Once you've sent your first connection request, mark this task as complete.

---

## Notes for Activity Setup

- Use a **Mark as complete** task type.
- This is the final task in the Pathway.
- Estimated time: 10–15 minutes.
- Encourage students to continue networking beyond this Pathway — building connections is an ongoing process.
- Remind them that this is just the start; they should aim to build their network gradually over their degree and beyond.

## Post-Pathway Guidance

Once students complete this Pathway:
- Encourage them to set a goal of adding a new connection every week or month
- Suggest they revisit and update their profile every semester with new projects and achievements
- Remind them to engage with content on LinkedIn (like, comment, share posts) to increase visibility
