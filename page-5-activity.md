# Page 5: Access LinkedIn — Activity

**Task Type:** Mark as complete

## Task

Log in to (or create) your LinkedIn account before moving on to the next page.

**Steps:**
- If you're new to LinkedIn: visit <a href="https://www.linkedin.com" target="_blank">linkedin.com</a> and sign up with your email.
- If you already have an account: log in and make sure your profile is accessible.
- Optional: customize your LinkedIn URL for easy sharing.

Once you're logged in and ready, mark this task as complete.

---

## Notes for Activity Setup

- Use a **Mark as complete** task type.
- This is a prerequisite for all subsequent pages — students must have access to LinkedIn before proceeding.
- Estimated time: 5–10 minutes (account creation or login).
- If students have account issues (e.g., locked account, verification email not received), suggest they contact LinkedIn Support or your institution's IT/careers support.
