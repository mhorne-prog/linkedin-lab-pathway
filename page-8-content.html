Add Your Experience, Projects and More

Think of this as your digital CV — but it's more than just a job history, it's also a portfolio. You should:

  • Quantify your impact — use numbers to show results (e.g., "managed a £50k budget", "led a team of 5", "delivered 3 weeks ahead of schedule")
  • Tailor your achievements to your desired roles — focus on skills and outcomes that matter for Project Management
  • Include projects — including coursework, and especially your final year project as it becomes clearer

How to Add a Project

Go to Profile → Add Section → Add Projects. Including projects is an essential part of being an employable Project Manager.

Pro Tip: Even student projects count. Describe the scope, your role, tools used, and the outcome. Recruiters want to see your practical experience.
