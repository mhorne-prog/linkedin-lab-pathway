# Page 8: Experience & Projects — Activity

**Task Type:** Mark as complete

## Task

Add at least one experience entry or project to your LinkedIn profile.

**What to add:**
- A past or current job/internship, OR
- A significant project (coursework, group project, final year project, personal project, etc.)

**Best practices:**
- Include quantified impact (numbers, metrics, outcomes)
- Describe your specific role and responsibilities
- Highlight skills relevant to Project Management
- Use action verbs (managed, led, delivered, implemented, etc.)

Once you've added at least one entry, mark this task as complete.

---

## Notes for Activity Setup

- Use a **Mark as complete** task type.
- Students should add content to their LinkedIn profile, then confirm they've done so.
- Estimated time: 15–20 minutes (writing and uploading one entry).
- Encourage students to add more entries over time as they complete new projects or roles.
- If students struggle to identify projects, remind them that coursework, group assignments, and internships all count.
