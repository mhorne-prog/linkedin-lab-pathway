# Page 1: Welcome — Activity

**Task Type:** Event Attendance / Mark Attendance

## Task

Mark your attendance at the **LinkedIn Lab for Project Managers** workshop/session.

By confirming your attendance, you acknowledge that you:
- Are ready to begin the LinkedIn Lab pathway
- Understand the 3 learning outcomes
- Commit to completing the pathway and building your professional LinkedIn profile

---

## Notes for Activity Setup

- Use an **Event Attendance** or **Mark Attendance** task type (if available in your Target Connect instance).
- This task creates a record that students attended the workshop/orientation session.
- Students simply need to confirm they were present and are ready to proceed.
- No responses or uploads required — just attendance confirmation.
- **Alternative:** If Target Connect doesn't support event attendance, fall back to "Mark as complete".
