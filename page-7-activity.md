# Page 7: Your About Section — Activity

**Task Type:** Text response

## Task

Draft your About section using the structure below, then add it to your LinkedIn profile.

**Structure:**
1. **Who you are** — your background and current role
2. **What you do best** — your key strengths and skills
3. **Key achievements** — quantified results or notable projects
4. **What opportunities you're looking for** — your career direction and ideal next step

**In your response, paste your full About section** (as it will appear on your LinkedIn profile).

---

## Notes for Activity Setup

- Use a **Text response** task type.
- Students should submit their full About section text.
- Estimated time: 20–30 minutes (drafting and refining).
- Expect responses of 200–300 words.
- Consider providing feedback on 2–3 example responses to show what makes a strong About section.
