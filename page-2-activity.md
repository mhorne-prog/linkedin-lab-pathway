# Page 2: Quick Poll — Activity

**Task Type:** Text response

## Task

Note down your answers to the three reflection questions:

1. Do you already have a LinkedIn account?
2. What do you think LinkedIn is useful for?
3. What questions do you have about LinkedIn?

---

## Notes for Activity Setup

- Use a **Text response** task type to allow students to write their answers.
- Students can provide a brief reflection — there's no "right" answer here.
- This sets up the motivation for the rest of the Pathway.
