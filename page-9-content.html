Your First Connections & Ongoing Project

Networking is a slow process — think less about the end result and more about the process and what you have in common with people.

Your first connections might be classmates, colleagues, or lecturers. From there, connect with experienced professionals, always sending a connection request with a personal note.

LinkedIn is your ongoing project — keep updating it with new projects and experience, and keep tailoring it towards your dream graduate role.
